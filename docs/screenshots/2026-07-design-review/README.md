# Design review screenshots, 17 July 2026

Captures of the homepage and key templates taken for the July design review,
split by the viewport they were shot at: `desktop/` at 1440x900 and 1038x684,
`mobile/` at 390x844. The two `*-full.png` files are whole-page captures.

They lived at the repo root until they were filed here. `.gitignore` has ignored
`/*.png` since 2026-07-11 precisely so scratch exports stop collecting there;
these predate the rule, which is why tracking them survived it.

**They are stale.** IA v2 landed on 2026-07-18, the day after these were taken,
and moved coolsculpting, onda and dermav to `/technology` and tattoo-removal to
`/concerns`. Read them as a record of what the design looked like at review, not
as a reference for what it looks like now. Nothing in the app links to them, so
they can be deleted whenever the review they document stops being interesting;
`git show 7722398` brings them back.
