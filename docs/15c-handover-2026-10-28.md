## 2. What Google has done so far

The full numbers are in `docs/15` §4.5. In short, over 28 Sep – 25 Oct against 25 Aug – 21 Sep (Malaysia):

- **Total clicks held** (850 → 874). The blog move is the one clear gain: blog clicks on both addresses went
  from 99 to 188.
- **The rewritten device pages gained impressions (+79%) but not position.** Their average position moved
  from 19.9 to 21.5. The rewrite has not yet earned better rankings.
- **None of the 14 pages that were not indexed on 24 Sep is indexed yet.** Google crawled six of them for the
  first time on 21 Oct, so it is now reading them. Five of the 14 are treatment pages that were completed only
  today, so Google has not seen their final form.

Our read: the pages are now as distinct as headings can make them, and that alone has not moved rankings. The
next test is paragraph-level repetition, which section 3.5 asks you about.
