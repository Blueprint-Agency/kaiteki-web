## What is Oligio Treatment?

Oligio treatment is a **non-surgical and non-invasive solution** for those looking to lift and tighten their skin without downtime. It’s a safe and effective option for anyone who wants visible results without needles or surgery.

The procedure works by using **Monopolar Radiofrequency (RF) energy**. This advanced technology stimulates your skin’s natural collagen production, which is the key to achieving firmness and elasticity.

What makes it unique is its ability to **deliver energy deep into the dermis** while safely passing through the outer layer of the skin. This means the treatment is both powerful and gentle at the same time.

The results? You’ll notice an **immediate tightening effect**, along with progressive improvements in skin elasticity over time. Your skin feels firmer, smoother, and more youthful.

## How Oligio Works: Technology & Methods

RF energy heats the skin layers up to 45–60°C:

Instant effect: Collagen fibers contract, giving immediate lifting.

Long-term effect: Stimulates fibroblasts to produce new collagen and elastin.

Equipped with an intelligent cooling system:

-   Protects the outer skin (epidermis) from burns.
-   Ensures patient comfort during the treatment.

• Targets deep skin layers, minimizing surface damage and preserving melanin, making it safe for all skin types.
