## What Is Profhilo Structura?

Profhilo Structura is an advanced injectable treatment designed to improve not just your skin, but the supporting layers beneath it. It is made with a high concentration of hyaluronic acid (a substance naturally found in our skin) using special NAHYCO®️ Hybrid Technology.

Unlike traditional fillers that add volume, Profhilo Structura works by stimulating your skin and tissues to regenerate themselves. It helps improve firmness, hydration, elasticity, and facial support — all while keeping your results natural and subtle.

Think of it as strengthening your skin from the inside out, rather than simply “filling” areas.

## How Is It Different from Regular Profhilo?

Both Profhilo treatments are excellent for skin rejuvenation, but they work at different levels:

**Classic Profhilo**

-   Improves skin hydration and glow
-   Smoothens fine lines
-   Ideal if your main concern is dull or dehydrated skin

**Profhilo Structura**

-   Works deeper within the face
-   Helps improve sagging, loss of firmness, and facial support
-   Enhances natural contours without looking puffy or overfilled
-   Suitable if you’re noticing facial drooping or volume loss

## How Does Profhilo Structura Work?

After being injected into specific areas, the hyaluronic acid spreads gently under the skin and encourages your body to:

-   Produce more collagen and elastin
-   Improve skin elasticity and firmness
-   Support superficial fat layers that contribute to youthful facial contours

Instead of sitting in one spot like fillers, Profhilo Structura integrates naturally with your tissues, allowing your face to look lifted, firmer, and refreshed over time.

## Who Is Profhilo Structura Suitable For?

This treatment is suitable if you:

-   Are starting to notice sagging or loss of firmness
-   Have mild to moderate volume loss in the cheeks or jawline
-   Want a natural lift without obvious filler volume
-   Prefer subtle, gradual improvement rather than dramatic change

## What Results Can You Expect?

Most patients experience:

-   Better hydration and glow within a few days
-   Smoother texture and firmer skin from 3–4 weeks
-   Gradual lifting and contour improvement over 6–8 weeks
-   A refreshed, youthful look that still looks like you

Results develop naturally, so changes feel soft and not sudden.

## Is It Safe? Are There Side Effects?

Profhilo Structura is generally very safe when performed by trained medical professionals.

You may experience mild, temporary effects such as:

-   -   Slight redness or small bumps at injection points
    -   Mild swelling or bruising
    -   Tenderness for a few days

## How Many Sessions Will I Need?

-   Most patients require 2 sessions, spaced 1 month apart
-   A third session may be advised at review, depending on your skin needs

Your doctor will customise the plan for optimal results.

## In Summary

Profhilo Structura is ideal for those who want natural-looking lifting, firmer skin, and improved facial support — without the heaviness or obvious look of traditional fillers. It’s a gentle yet powerful way to restore youthful structure and skin quality with minimal downtime.
