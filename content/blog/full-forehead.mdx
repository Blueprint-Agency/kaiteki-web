## What Causes Brow and Forehead Descent?

**Aging :**

This is caused by the aging process that occurs naturally. As we age, the collagen and elastin in our face will start to diminish. With gravity and the diminished collagen, the skin will start to descend and become saggy, giving that ‘ wrinkled’ look. Of course, due to the bone resorption which I wrote about it here, your forehead will be less pronounced too as  you age.

**Dehydrated Skin:**

We know how vital water is to your body; it is also crucial for the skin to be hydrated enough to strengthen and repair its barrier functions. Too much water loss and inadequate water supply to the body can cause dehydration and affects the skin to be more sensitive, dull-looking and display accelerated signs of ageing like fine lines.

**Weight Loss :**

It is known that individuals who are heavier have more fats distributed and a result of that, the skin is stretched.  A sudden weight loss will contribute to the fat layer being burned off and all that is left will be just saggy skin.

**Free radicals :**

Free radicals reactive oxygen species are generated by our body by various endogenous systems or exposure to different physiochemical conditions. A balance between free radicals and antioxidants is necessary for proper physiological function. If free radicals overwhelm the body’s ability to regulate them, a condition known as oxidative stress ensues. Thus, free radicals cause oxidative damage to the key proteins like elastin and collagen hence speeding up the aging process of the skin and forming wrinkles. Examples that contribute to free radicals are like smoking, UV light, certain type of foods.
