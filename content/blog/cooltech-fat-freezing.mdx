## What Is CoolTech Fat Freezing?

**Unwanted bulges** contain fat cells, which can be resistant to diet and exercise but not to **cryolipolysis**. CoolTech Fat Freezing uses controlled cooling and gentle suction to remove heat from targeted fat tissue. The fat is cooled just below freezing, causing crystals to form inside the fat cells. This triggers a natural process called **apoptosis**, where fat cells begin to die within 2–3 days after treatment.

As the fat cells collapse, the body’s **inflammatory response** activates, gradually removing the dead cells through the **lymphatic system**. Over the next **30 to 90 days**, the body naturally eliminates these fat cells, leading to a visible **reduction in the fat layer**. The released fat is safely processed by the **liver and used as energy**, leaving the treated area **slimmer and more contoured.**

CoolTech targets and freezes fat cells without damaging nerves or other tissues, ensuring that only the fat cells are affected during the process. Moreover, to ensure the safety of surrounding tissue during the process, a **cryoprotectant membrane, the Cool Gel Pad (Patented RFID)**, is employed. This membrane contains glycerol, which serves as a cryopreservative for skin cells, preventing the formation of ice crystals and minimizing the potential of cellular damage.

The treatment area is sanitised, marked and prepared with a cooling gel pad before applicators are applied.
