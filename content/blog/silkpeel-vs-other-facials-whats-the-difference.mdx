## What is SilkPeel?

SilkPeel is a facial that involves a unique 3-in-1 treatment, using patented technology that simultaneously exfoliates, extracts, and infuses skin with condition specific serums. The machine is equipped with a diamond tip which physically exfoliates dead skin cells, the dead cells are then being sucked away by a closed loop vacuum system while simultaneously infusing a serum to the skin. This results in greater serum penetration, absorption, and retention as the dead cells are not blocking the way, providing a long-lasting result as compared to ordinary facials.

## SilkPeel vs Other Facials

**1\. Vs Chemical Peels**

**Similarities:** they both targets deeper skin concerns such as acne, fine lines, dullness, and hyperpigmentation.

**Differences:** Chemical peel will have higher risk of irritation, especially for patients with sensitive/ rosacea skin. At the same time, it will also benefit more for those who has severe acne and acne scars, typically require a few days of downtime.

**2\. Vs Hydrafacial/ Hydroglow**

**Similarities:** Both provides a boost in the skin hydration level, giving you immediate plumped and glowing skin with no downtime.

**Differences:** Silkpeel typically last longer than a Hydrafacial due to the deeper depth of treatment by SilkPeel. It provides exfoliation manually while Hydrafacial uses mild chemical exfoliant.

**3\. Vs Kaiteki Rest & Relax Facial:**

**Similarities:** Safe for all skin types with no downtime.

**Differences:** SilkPeel provides manual exfoliation with medical grade serum infusion, therefore it can help with various skin concerns such as pigmentation and fine lines. Kaiteki Rest & Relax facial is perfect for those who wants a very relaxing facial with no major skin issues.
