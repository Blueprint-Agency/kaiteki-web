## Introduction

These days, we are overwhelmed by the choices of skinboosters in the market. Here’s a non sponsored guide for you to explore the choices at Kaiteki Clinic to address different skin concerns.

When it comes to skinboosters, hyaluronic acid is the primary ingredient choice. It helps achieve skin glow, combat early signs of aging and adding moisture & elasticity.

Profhilo and Skinvive represent two leading Hyaluronic acid based skin boosters, each with its unique application method and areas of injections, catering to a diverse range of patient needs and preferences.

## Profhilo

Renowned for Bio Aesthetic Points (BAP) technique, which strategically targets five injection points on each side of the face to maximize spread of hyaluronic acid with minimal injections

Suitable for patients who are fearful of injections as less number of injection points is required

## Skinvive

Formulated with crossed link hyaluronic acid, it has higher longevity ( up to 9 months) and is usually delivered via microinjections. This allows more precised and targeted treatment to address specific areas of concern especially for fine lines and open pores.

Suitable for patients who are seeking for a more tailored treatment plan and long-lasting moisture retention.

Rejuran/Plinest are PN-based injectables which ate known for their healing and anti-inflammatory benefits, making them an excellent option for sensitive or ageing skin.

K whitening is an in-house blend of Hyaluronic acid, Ascorbic Acid, Glutathione, Superoxide Dismutase, Lipoic Acid, Tranexemic Acid. This 3.5cc injectable packed with antioxidants helps to hydrate skin from within, reduces pigmentation and protects skin from oxidative stress.
