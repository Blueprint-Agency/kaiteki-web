## Introduction

Skin ageing is a gradual process, but early care can make a significant difference. **Preventive aging** focuses on maintaining skin health, firmness, and collagen before visible signs of ageing become established.

At **Kaiteki Aesthetic Clinic**, we believe prevention is the foundation of natural, long-lasting skin results.

## What Is Skin Ageing?

Skin ageing is influenced by both **internal** and **external** factors:

-    **Intrinsic ageing**: Genetics and natural biological changes
-    **Extrinsic ageing**: UV exposure, pollution, stress, and lifestyle habits

Many patients experience early ageing signs earlier than expected, often in **their late 20s or early 30s**.

This is why **preventive** aging plays an important role even before wrinkles or sagging become visible.

## Daily Habits That Accelerate Skin Ageing

Certain daily habits contribute significantly to premature skin ageing:

-    Insufficient sun protection
-    Poor sleep quality and prolonged stress
-    Smoking and inadequate hydration
-    Inconsistent or unsuitable skincare routines

Clinically, these factors are frequently observed in patients who seek treatment for **premature ageing concerns.**

## Early Signs You Should Not Ignore

Early skin ageing does not always appear dramatically

Subtle changes may include:

-   Fine lines and dull, tired- looking skin tone
-   Uneven skin texture or pigmentation
-   Reduced hydration and skin elasticity

Addressing these signs early allows treatments to be more **effective, gentle, and natural-looking**, helping to preserve overall skin quality in the long term.

## Why Preventive Aging Matters

Preventive ageing focuses on **maintaining collagen, skin structure, and overall skin health**, rather than repairing advanced damage. This approach slows visible ageing, enhances skin resilience, and supports graceful ageing over time.

From a medical perspective:

-    Slows visible ageing
-    Enhances skin resilience
-    Reduces the need for aggressive corrective treatments later

It is a **proactive approach to ageing** subtle, refined, and sustainable
