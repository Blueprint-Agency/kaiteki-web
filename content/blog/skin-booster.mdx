## Introduction

In general, we start losing collagen and elastin in our mid twenties. Our epidermis becomes thinner and signs of aging begin to show on our face and neck. Hence, it is never too early to start on skin booster.

## What is skin booster?

Skin booster is commonly known as ‘水光针’ in mandarin. As the name suggests, skin booster is a product injected into the skin via microneedles to give the skin a boost– of hydration and rejuvenation. They also stimulate collagen & elastin production at cellular level thus reducing signs of aging.

## What is skin booster made of?

Skin boosters are usually made of Hyaluronic Acid (HA). Some brands may have added composition with other collagen stimulating ingredients.

Hyaluronic acid is a component that may also be found naturally in the skin. It is capable of holding 1000 times its molecular weight in water.  Naturally occurring hyaluronic acid gives our skin a more radiant, healthy and supple.

## I am already using high grade skincare products, do I still need skin booster?

If you are still unsatisfied with the results from your skincare products, you can consider trying skin booster. Skin booster bypasses the skin barrier, and are injected precisely into the deeper layers of the skin. By putting hyaluronic acid and other anti-aging ingredients in the dermis, you do not need to worry of its absorption or efficacy.

## How does it work?

With its high affinity to water molecules, studies have shown that Hyaluronic Acid not only helps improves skin hydration from within. It also helps to

-   restore brighter and radiant complexion
-   improve skin elasticity
-   reduce fine lines & wrinkles
-   improve scars
-   reduce trans-epidermal water loss
