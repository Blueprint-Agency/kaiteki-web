## FAQ's

**Is Plinest Injection Treatment suitable for all skin types?**

People who are experiencing premature skin aging with several skin conditions such as skin sensitivity, enlarged pores, oily skin, wrinkles, and acne. PN treatment would be suitable for those who wish to address several skin issues at the same time. In addition, people who have acne scars also achieve good wound healing results with PN treatment.

**How many sessions do I need?**

For first-timers, we recommend that you undergo a programme of 4 sessions, with each session around 4 weeks apart. After that, you can opt for a session every 3-6 months to maintain the results of the treatment.

**How long is the plinest injection procedure?**

The whole process will take about 30 minutes. Doctor will use either cannula or direct injection to administer the product (highly purified PN) into the skin, there is only minimal discomfort during the treatment.

**Are there any potential side effects or precautions to consider?**

You may experience some skin redness and mild swelling and possibly bruising too. The downtime normally lasted for 3-5 days depending on individual. We advice to stop taking supplements or blood thinner 3 days prior to treatment.

**How soon can results be expected with Plinest injection?**

Collagen and fibroblast stimulation and proliferation normally takes time. The result will not show immediately post treatment. Generally, it takes about 1-2 weeks after the first treatment and better results can be seen after several sessions.
