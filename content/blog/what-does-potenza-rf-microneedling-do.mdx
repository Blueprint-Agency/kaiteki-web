## How Radiofrequency and Microneedling work for your skin?

The Potenza Microneedling system is a minimally invasive device delivering monopolar or bipolar non-ablative RF energy to the dermal layers of the skin.

The handpiece inserts an array of microneedles which penetrate into the tissue and radiofrequency (RF) is delivered through the microneedles heating the targeted area.

Clinical studies suggest that these thermal effects from RF energy cause **constriction and tightening of the skin through soft tissue coagulation**, as well as **stimulation of collagen and elastin production**.

As time goes on, the newly produced collagen contributes to improved skin elasticity and overall skin texture, ultimately reducing the visibility of various skin concerns like large pores, fine lines, loose skin, age spots and acne scars.

## How long will this treatment last?

Treatment time may vary, however most treatments are **approximately 30-45 minutes**.
