## Introduction

The BTL Exilis Elite is a FDA approved, non-surgical treatment for skin laxity on the face and body. It uses a combination of monopolar radio frequency (RF) energy and ultrasound to stimulate fat lipolysis (the breakdown of fat cells) and collagen remodelling (for skin tightening) to contour the body and face.

Its popularity stems from its efficacy and minimal downtime, making it a preferred choice for individuals seeking non-surgical cosmetic enhancements.

## What Is Exilis Elite?

It is a non-invasive treatment to tone your skin and body. The dual energy delivered by Exilis Elite speeds up the metabolic activity of fat cells and causes them to shrink; at the same time it also stimulates and strengthens the collagen network which improves skin laxity and texture.

## What to Expect During Treatment

### Step 1: Consultation

Doctors at Kaiteki Clinic will first consult and understand your areas of concern (eg: face, neck, tummy, arms, or thighs) in order to customize a treatment plan for you.

### Step 2: Treatment

During the session, a small handheld device gently warms the skin. Patients will feel a warming sensation as the energy is delivered to the deeper layers of skin at a targeted depth.

It’s recommended to do every 1 to 2 weeks with a minimum of 4 to 6 treatments. You may see visible improvements after a single session. Results will typically improve further over a 3-month follow up period.

## Who Is Exilis Elite For?

This treatment is great for people who struggle with stubborn fat areas like double chin, tummy, arms, or thighs. The mechanical effect of ultrasound helps create separation of the fat cells clusters, allowing more efficient RF thermal delivery to adipose tissue.

It is also beneficial for those who have mild to moderate skin looseness, especially after pregnancy or weight changes. Exilis Elite helps in shrinking of loose tissue immediately, as well as long term collagen production that lifts and tightens loose skin over time.

## Why Exilis Ultra Is Different

Exilis is the only monopolar RF (Radiofrequency) system to incorporate ultrasonic energy in one hand piece. It is used to tighten sagging skin of the face and used in body contouring treatments to reduce fat and tighten loose skin. This non-invasive treatment offers cosmetic improvement without the downtime of surgery.
