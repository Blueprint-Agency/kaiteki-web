## Do I still need to apply topical SPF if I’m taking this?

Some people may find topical sunscreen too greasy and tend to clog up the pores. Unfortunately, oral sunblock cannot replace your regular topical sunblock. For maximum protection, it is recommended to use topical sunscreen in conjunction with your oral sunblock.

At least for the face, it is important to also apply topical sunscreen, but when combined with oral sunblock, you can go for lighter products (SPF15-30) to reduce the risk of clogging pores.
