## What is Juvederm Filler?

Juvederm range of **[dermal fillers](/blog/dermal-fillers)** are celebrated for its ability to enhance facial aesthetics. It is composed of hyaluronic acid, a substance naturally found in the skin.

The smooth gel filler is designed to add volume and hydration to targeted areas. It is widely used for lip enhancement due to its natural look and feel.

## How Does Juvederm Filler Work for Lips?

### Procedure Details

A numbing cream is usually applied beforehand to minimise the discomfort. After that, the medical practitioner will carefully inject the filler to achieve your desired look.

The procedure usually takes about 15-30 minutes.

Common downtimes: Mild swelling, redness and some bruising, which will usually subside within a few days.

Aftercare instructions: Avoid hot drinks, excessive pressure over the lips, alcohol, strenuous exercise for at least 24-48 hours

## Benefits of Juvederm Filler for Lips

The hyaluronic acid in Juvederm also hydrates the lips, giving them a smooth, supple and soft texture that feels as good as it looks. This is especially beneficial for lips prone to dryness.

## Is Juvederm Filler Safe for Lips?

### Safety Considerations

Juvederm is FDA-approved and considered safe for use on lips when administered by a licensed and experienced professional. It is minimally invasive and well-tolerated by most-individuals.

### Common Side Effects

Temporary side effects include swelling, mild bruising and redness, which typically resolve within a few days. Application of cold compress during the first few days will help to manage the discomfort.
