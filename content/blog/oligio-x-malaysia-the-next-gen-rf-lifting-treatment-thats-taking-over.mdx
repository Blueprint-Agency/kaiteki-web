## Introduction

Oligio X is the next-gen, needle-less skin treatment using advanced radiofrequency (RF) energy to wake up your collagen, tighten your skin, and smooth out wrinkles, all with close to zero downtime. Think firmer, fresher, more youthful skin in a quick, non-invasive session.

## What is Oligio X

1.   It is the latest evolution of the original Oligio treatment, upgraded with dual mode (G-mode + X-mode) RF system for deeper, more customisable results.
2.   It is FDA-cleared monopolar RF device developed in Korea by Wontech company that deliver adjustable high power energy ~400W (6.78Mhz) delivered in controlled pulses deep into dermis to safely heat the skin and trigger collagen remodeling.
3.   **X-mode**: target deep firming
    **G-mode**: target surface rejuvenation
4.   It also provide real-time intelligent cooling system (up to ~11 pulses per shot) forcomfort and burn prevention

## What Skin Concerns Does Oligio X Treat

1.  Sagging skin on (face, jawline/jowl and neck)
2.  Fine lines and wrinkles (nasolabial fold, forehead and fine lines around eyes)
3.  Poor skin quality (enlarged pores, uneven skin texture and tone)
4.  Lack of facial definition (early jowling and droopy eyelids, blurred jawline, mild double chin)

## What to Expect During Your Oligio X session?

1.  Consultation and skin assessment
2.  Doctor will examine your face and skin condition followed by discuss your desired result and treatment areas. Doctor will sets custom energy level and depth based on your skin type.
3.  Cleansing and preparation
4.  Skin is cleaned to remove makeup, oil and dirt.
5.  A thin layer of conductive gel may be applied to help RF energy penetrate evenly
6.  Treatment usually takes 30–60 minutes depending on the area.
7.   You will feel:
    -   Warmth or mild tingling but not painful in the treated area.
    -   Slight snapping sensation as collagen contracts.
    -   No anaesthesia needed. Numbing cream application is optional.
8.  After care
    -   Doctor will examine the skin for redness or irritation. Usually, the skin may be slightly pink for 1–2 hours.

## Frequency Ask Question

### 1\. Is Oligio X painful?

Most of our patient felt minimal discomfort (typically warmth or mild tingling sensation). The built in cooling system keeps the experience comfortable.

### 2\. How many sessions do I need?

Most patient see result after 1 session. However, a series of 3-4 sessions (4-6 weeks apart) is recommended for optimal outcomes.

### 3\. Can I combine Oligio X with other treatments?

Yes. Oligio X works perfectly well with dermal fillers, botox, laser resurfacing, ultherapy and RF microneedling and skin boosters for enhanced results.

### 4\. Is there any downtime after Oligio X?

Minimal to zero downtime. You can return to usual activities immediately after the session.

### 5\. How long do results last?

Results of a single Oligio X session typically last up about 9 -12 month, with collagen gradually building over 2-3 months post treatment.
