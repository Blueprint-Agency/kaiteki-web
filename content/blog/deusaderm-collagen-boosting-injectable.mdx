## Introduction

If you’ve been searching for something between skincare and filler — something that revives rather than just fills — meet **Deusaderm**.

## What is Deusaderm?

[Deusaderm](/technology/deusaderm) is an injectable gel of cross-linked type I collagen, purified from pig (porcine) skin by Sunmax Biotechnology in Taiwan. The version supplied in Malaysia also contains lidocaine, a local anaesthetic, and it contains no hyaluronic acid.

The collagen is processed to remove the end segments of the molecule most associated with allergic reactions, but because it is animal-derived, your doctor will still take an allergy history. If a porcine product is not acceptable to you for religious, dietary or personal reasons, say so: non-animal options can be discussed instead.

Rather than prompting collagen with a synthetic particle, Deusaderm places collagen itself, giving some support when injected before it is absorbed over the following months. The manufacturer also describes it as supporting the skin’s own collagen over time; the published studies measured wrinkle grading and skin readings rather than new collagen directly, so treat that as the manufacturer’s account.

This means:

-   It does not bind water the way hyaluronic acid does, which is why doctors consider it where puffiness is a concern
-   It is not swelling-free: swelling, redness, tenderness and bruising are common in the first days, especially under the eyes
-   It is temporary, and it cannot be dissolved if a lump or an unwanted result occurs

## What Makes It Different from Traditional Fillers?

| Feature | Traditional Filler (e.g. HA) | Deusaderm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Texture | Clear gel | White, creamy gel |
| Purpose | Fill and volumize | Lighter support, under-eye area, fine wrinkles |
| Action | Volume when injected | Some support when injected, absorbed over months |
| Absorption | Absorbs water (may cause swelling and puffiness) | Non-hydrophilic, though injection swelling is still common |
| Risk | Tyndall effect (blue cast under eyes) | Opaque, so no Tyndall effect was seen in the 2024 trial |
| Dissolvable | Yes, with hyaluronidase | No, so placement has to be conservative |
| Longevity | 6–12 months, depending type and area | Temporary; varies with the area and technique |

**In short:**

*Filler binds water. Deusaderm does not.*

## What Is It Used For? (Indications)

Deusaderm is commonly considered for:

-   Hollowness along the tear trough and lower eyelid
-   Dark circles linked to thin skin and hollowing
-   Crepey, lax skin around the eyes
-   Concern about looking overfilled after repeated hyaluronic acid filler
-   Mild, localised volume loss where a lighter correction is preferred

Under-eye darkness has several causes, including pigment, visible blood vessels and hollowing, and an injectable addresses only some of them, so your doctor assesses the cause first. A small, uncontrolled 2024 study of Sunmax’s collagen around the eyes, funded by the manufacturer, recorded a change in skin lightness readings that had faded by 12 weeks.

## The Procedure: What to Expect

The procedure is quick and tailored to your skin’s needs:

**1\. Deep Injection:**
Aims to provide support, especially for hollow under-eyes or mild volume loss.

**2\. Superficial Injection:**
Placed more superficially for thin skin and surface texture. Both techniques can be done using a needle or cannula, depending on the treatment area and your comfort.

-   Treatment Time: ~30 minutes
-   Downtime: Most people return to normal activity the same day, but swelling, redness and bruising are common in the first days, especially under the eyes, so allow a margin before any event that matters to you

## Side Effects

As with any injectable, Deusaderm carries risks, which are explained during consultation. Side effects may include:

-   Redness or swelling, which tends to show more under the eyes
-   Bruising at the injection site
-   Tenderness or pain
-   Small lumps (nodules), itching or skin flaking

In a 2024 trial of Sunmax’s collagen, these effects were recorded mostly at the injection site, and most resolved within a month. Allergic reaction and infection are possible with any animal-derived implant, and rare but serious vascular complications are recognised for facial injectables in general. Unlike hyaluronic acid filler, collagen cannot be dissolved with hyaluronidase, so careful assessment and conservative placement matter.

## Is Deusaderm Right for You?

-   It may suit hollowness or an overfilled look under the eyes, where its lack of water-binding and opaque colour matter
-   It is made from pig collagen, so tell your doctor if a porcine product is not acceptable to you, or if you have any allergy to collagen, pork-derived products or lidocaine
-   It is not used in pregnancy or while breastfeeding, or where there is infection or inflammation in the area
-   It is temporary, and it cannot be dissolved
