## Introduction

If you’ve been searching for something between skincare and filler — something that revives rather than just fills — meet **Deusaderm**.

## What is Deusaderm?

Deusaderm is a gel collagen biostimulator, derived from purified porcine (pig) collagen.

*“Porcine-derived collagen has been safely used in medicine for decades — in wound healing, dental procedures, and even heart surgery. It’s* *a trusted, proven material in the medical field.”*

Unlike traditional fillers that simply add volume, Deusaderm works by nourishing your skin — giving it the nutrients needed for natural collagen synthesis without triggering inflammation.

This means:

-   No puffiness
-   No swelling
-   Just progressive, natural-looking skin regeneration

## What Makes It Different from Traditional Fillers?

| Feature | Traditional Filler (e.g. HA) | Deusaderm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Texture | Clear gel | White, creamy gel |
| Purpose | Fill and volumize | Regenerate and brighten |
| Action | Instant volume | Gradual skin improvement |
| Absorption | Absorbs water (may cause swelling and puffiness) | Non-hydrophilic (no swelling or puffiness) |
| Risk | Tyndall effect (blue cast under eyes) | No Tyndall effect |
| Longevity | 6–12 months, depending type and area | Build collagen gradually, long-term improvement |

**In short:**

*Filler fills. Deusaderm heals.*

## What Is It Best Used For? (Indications)

Deusaderm is ideal for:

-   Eye rejuvenation
-   Tear trough correction
-   Dark circles, especially those caused by pigmentation or skin thinning
-   Structural support, volume loss

It also helps reduce melanin production, which results in brighter, more even-toned skin — making it both corrective and preventative.

## The Procedure: What to Expect

The procedure is quick and tailored to your skin’s needs:

**1\. Deep Injection:**
Provides structural support, especially for hollow under-eyes or mild volume loss.

**2\. Superficial Injection:**
Targets brightness, skin tone, and surface texture. Both techniques can be done using a needle or cannula, depending on the treatment area and your comfort.

-   Treatment Time: ~30 minutes
-   Downtime: Minimal (mild redness or swelling may occur and resolve quickly

## Side Effects

As with any injectable, minor side effects may include:

-   Mild redness or swelling
-   Small bruising at the injection site
-   Tenderness for a day or two

Because Deusaderm is non-hydrophilic and doesn’t trigger inflammation, it’s known for being
gentle, especially in sensitive areas like the under-eyes.
