## Everything You Need to Know About This Innovative Skin Treatment

In the ever-evolving world of non-invasive aesthetic treatments, **Onda Face** has emerged as a cutting-edge option for those seeking firmer, younger-looking skin without surgery or downtime.

Whether you’re exploring skin tightening solutions or just curious about the latest in beauty tech, this post covers everything you need to know about Onda Face—from how it works to what results to expect.

## What is Onda Face?

**Onda Face** is a non-surgical facial rejuvenation treatment that uses **Coolwaves® microwave technology** to target skin laxity, fine lines, and sagging in the face and neck. Developed by the Italian company **DEKA**, Onda (meaning “wave” in Italian) is the same platform known for its body sculpting capabilities—but with a smaller applicator designed for delicate facial areas.

It offers an alternative to more invasive procedures like facelifts or laser treatments by delivering controlled microwaves deep into the skin layers to stimulate collagen production and tighten existing tissue.

## How Does Onda Face Work?

Onda Face harnesses **Coolwaves®**, a patented microwave energy (at 2.45 GHz) that precisely penetrates the dermis and subdermal layers. Here’s how it works:

Firstly, before we start, we will apply a layer of thin oil so the applicator can glide on the face more seamlessly.

1.  **Microwave energy** heats the deeper layers of the skin while keeping the surface cool, thanks to an integrated cooling system in the applicator.
2.  The heat stimulates **new collage and** tightens connective tissue thus improving skin elasticity.
3.  Over time, this results in smoother, firmer, and more youthful-looking skin.

The treatment is often described as comfortable, with most patients feeling a warm sensation during the session. There’s not much of down time.

## Who is Suitable for Onda Face?

Onda Face is suitable for individuals who:

-   Have **mild to moderate skin laxity** (sagging) on the face or neck
-   Want a **non-invasive** treatment with no downtime
-   Are seeking to **boost collagen** and improve skin tone
-   Are not ready for or not interested in surgical options

It works well for all skin types and tones, making it a versatile option. However, it may not be ideal for individuals with severe sagging or significant fat deposits in the treated areas.

## What Are the Desired Results?

Patients can expect:

-   **Tighter, firmer skin** on the face and jawline
-   **Improved skin texture and tone**
-   Reduction in **fine lines and early wrinkles**
-   A more **contoured facial appearance**

The effect is often described as a **natural lift** rather than a dramatic change. It’s subtle yet noticeable, especially over a series of treatments.

**How Long Does It Take to See Results?**

-   **Initial results** can be noticed after the **first session**, especially in skin tone and texture.
-   **Optimal results** typically appear after **3–4 sessions**, spaced 2 to 4 weeks apart.
-   **Full collagen regeneration** continues for up to **3 months** after the final treatment.

The exact timeline may vary based on individual skin condition, age, and overall skin health.

## Are There Any Side Effects?

Onda Face is generally well tolerated with **minimal side effects**. Possible mild reactions include:

-   Temporary **redness** or warmth in the treated area
-   Slight **swelling** or **tingling**
-   Very rarely, **mild bruising**

There is **no downtime**, so patients can return to normal activities immediately after treatment. As always, it’s important to consult with a qualified provider who can assess your skin and discuss any risks specific to your health.
