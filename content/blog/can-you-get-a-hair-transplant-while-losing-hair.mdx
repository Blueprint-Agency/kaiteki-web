## Understanding Hair Loss and Hair Transplant.

Hair loss is more than just a physical phenomenon; its an emotional journey that can evoke feelings of insecurity, self-consciousness, and even depression in some individuals.

So, as I have mention that genetic do play a role in this, which known as male – pattern baldness (androgenic alopecia) in men and female patient, which typically starting at the temples or crown of the head.

**Other factors such as:**

-   **Hormonal Changes:** Hormonal imbalance can contribute to hair loss, such as pregnancy, childbirth , menopause, thyroid disorders and hormonal fluctuation.
-   **Medical Conditions:** Certain medical conditions can cause hair loss as symptom, such as alopecia areata (an autoimmune disorder that causes patchy hair loss), scalp infection, trichotillomania (a compulsive hair-pulling disorder) and underlying health disorder such as Lupus or Diabetes.
-   **Medication and Treatments:** Chemotherapy drugs, antidepressants, blood thinners, and medications for high blood pressure are among those known to cause hair shedding. Radiation therapy can also result in temporary or permanent hair loss in the treated area.
-   **Nutritional Deficiencies:** Inadequate intake of essential nutrients, particularly iron, zinc, protein, and vitamins (such as Vitamin D, Vitamin B12 and biotin), can affect hair growth and lead to hair loss.
-   **Age:** As people age, the rate of hair growth slows down, and hair follicles may become finer and less dense.

Beside treating those reversible causes above, hair transplant surgery offers a beacon of hope for those struggling with hair loss, providing a permanent solution that restores not only hair but also confidence.

Through meticulous surgical technique, hair transplant surgeons can harvest hair follicles from donor areas, typically the back or sides of scalps, and transplant them into the areas where hair loss has occurred.

## Can you get a hair transplant while losing hair?

Getting hair transplant while losing hair is definitely the greatest idea especially for individual who experience hair loss. As it not just only able to help to restore the hair density on the specific area that is thinned or receded, which provide you with a fuller and more youthful appearance.

Modern hair transplant techniques, such as **Follicular Unit Transplantation (FUT)** or **Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE)**, results in very natural – looking hair growth.

A very skilled surgeons carefully transplant hair follicles to mimic the natural hair pattern of hair growth, and ensuring there is seamless integration with existing hair. By doing this, will significantly improve on the self – esteem and confidence, which somewhat able to improve overall quality of life for many individuals.

While hair transplant surgery offers numerous benefits, there are also some potential challenges and consideration that individuals should be aware of before undergoing the procedure. As the success rate of hair transplant really depends on the availability of an adequate donor hair supply.

In some cases, some individuals may not have enough donor hair to achieve their desired results, especially if they have advanced hair loss. It is common for transplanted hair to undergo a shedding phase in the weeks following surgery. The shedding is normal part of hair growth cycle and typically precedes the growth of new, permanent hair.

And sometimes transplanted hair may not be able to keep up with the ongoing hair loss in certain area, therefore a must to discuss with your medical practitioner before perform hair transplant. Sometimes we could perform topical or injection treatment first before hair transplant surgery treatment.

Other than that healthy scalp condition and also extent of hair loss also play an important role to justify on the success rate of it. Poor scalp health for example with frequent hair scalp acne condition or frequent hair fungal infection might not yield a good outcome.

Although, there is not strict age limit for hair transplant surgery, patient should at least do it before it become extensive hair loss situation or earlier age, which might have good quantity of donor hair and also reduce on the recovery period of the hair transplant surgery
