## What is Density Treatment?

**Density** is an innovative aesthetic treatment that uses **radiofrequency energy** to heat the deeper layers of the skin, thereby **stimulating collagen and elastin production**. Any firming builds gradually over the following months, and results vary between individuals.

The system combines **Monopolar and Bipolar RF modes** in a single platform, allowing for customized treatment depths and energy delivery based on the patient’s skin condition and goals.

Whether you’re concerned about facial sagging, skin laxity around the neck, or loss of elasticity post-pregnancy, Density offers a non-invasive option, though the published studies on it so far are small and uncontrolled.

## How Density Treatments Work: Technologies & Methods

### Dual RF Technology:

-   **Monopolar RF**: Delivers heat deeper into the subcutaneous tissue, ideal for skin tightening and contouring.
-   **Bipolar RF**: Targets the superficial dermis, perfect for improving fine lines, pores, and overall skin texture.

### Key Features:

-   **Real-time impedance monitoring** to ensure safe, even energy delivery.
-   **Cryogen cooling** that protects the skin surface while heat builds beneath it.
-   **Multiple treatment handpieces** to treat various areas: face, under-eye, neck, abdomen, arms, and thighs.

By delivering controlled thermal energy to the dermis and subdermal layers, the treatment **triggers natural collagen remodeling** while preserving the skin’s surface.

### Versatile Applications:

-   Can be combined with microneedling, PRP, HIFU, or laser therapies for synergistic results, depending on the indication.

## Who is it For & What Can It Treat?

Density treatment is suitable for both men and women seeking **non-surgical skin rejuvenation**. It suits people with mild to moderate laxity who prefer an option without needles and with little downtime.

**Treatment Areas & Benefits:**

-   Mild to moderate laxity of the jawline, cheeks and neck
-   Tightening mildly loose skin (for example postpartum); marked sagging is generally beyond any surface RF device
-   Reducing fine lines and wrinkles
-   Minimizing enlarged pores
-   Smoothing uneven skin texture
-   Improving mild skin laxity on the body

Because RF heats tissue rather than targeting pigment, it can be considered **across skin tones**, making it a versatile option for patients of various ethnicities and skin types, though settings still need to be matched to your skin.

## Results, Safety & Recovery

### When Will I See Results?

-   **A slight tightening** may be noticed soon after a session, as existing collagen contracts.
-   **Collagen production continues** over the next 4-6 months, leading to progressive skin improvement.
-   Typically, **one to two sessions per year** are recommended, spaced **6 to 12 months apart**, depending on the number of shots delivered and your doctor’s professional assessment.

### Safety & Side Effects:

-   Holds US FDA 510(k) clearance as a radiofrequency device for dermatologic procedures, which is a regulatory clearance of the device, not an approval of any cosmetic outcome.
-   Redness is expected and mild swelling is common, both usually short-lived. Less commonly, RF can cause blistering, a burn or a change in pigmentation, which is why settings are matched to your skin by a trained doctor.
-   Most people return to normal activities the same day.
