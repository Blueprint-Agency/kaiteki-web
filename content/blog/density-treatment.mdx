## What is Density Treatment?

**Density** is an innovative aesthetic treatment that uses **radiofrequency energy** to heat the deeper layers of the skin, thereby **stimulating collagen and elastin production**. The result is visibly firmer, smoother skin with improved tone and texture over time.

The system combines **Monopolar and Bipolar RF modes** in a single platform, allowing for customized treatment depths and energy delivery based on the patient’s skin condition and goals.

Whether you’re concerned about facial sagging, skin laxity around the neck, or loss of elasticity post-pregnancy, Density offers a non-invasive solution with proven results.

## How Density Treatments Work: Technologies & Methods

### Dual RF Technology:

-   **Monopolar RF**: Delivers heat deeper into the subcutaneous tissue, ideal for skin tightening and contouring.
-   **Bipolar RF**: Targets the superficial dermis, perfect for improving fine lines, pores, and overall skin texture.

### Key Features:

-   **Real-time impedance monitoring** to ensure safe, even energy delivery.
-   **Temperature control system** to prevent overheating or burns.
-   **Multiple treatment handpieces** to treat various areas: face, under-eye, neck, abdomen, arms, and thighs.

By delivering controlled thermal energy to the dermis and subdermal layers, the treatment **triggers natural collagen remodeling** while preserving the skin’s surface.

### Versatile Applications:

-   Can be combined with microneedling, PRP, HIFU, or laser therapies for synergistic results, depending on the indication.
