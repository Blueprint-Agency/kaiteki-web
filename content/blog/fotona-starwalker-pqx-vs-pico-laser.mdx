## Understanding the Technologies

### What is Fotona StarWalker PQX?

Fotona Starwalker PQX is Multi-wavelength **Ultra-short Pico** pulses technology laser in which using **Shortest Pico pulses (≈ 300 picoseconds)** and **highest peak power (~2.7 GW)** and that place the StarWalker PQX at the pinnacle of Pico technology. Besides that, it is using Third – generation **Adaptive Structured Pulse (ASP)** tech for smarter, safer energy delivery.

Fotona Star walker PQX equipped with **FracTat ®**—a patented ablative + non-ablative format for micro-hole creation, boosting pigment and tattoo clearance. It targets pigmentation, melasma, scars and skin rejuvenation. It is safe for most skin types because of the customizable settings for precision.

### What is Pico Laser?

Pico Laser are purely picosecond technology delivering very short pulses in which it breaks down pigment into tiny particles for faster clearance.

It is effective for pigmentations, acne scars, tattoo removal and skin brightening as well. It comes with different variant of brands include PicoSure, PicoPlus, and PicoWay.
