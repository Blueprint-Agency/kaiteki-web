## The Impact on Facial Aesthetics

One of the most noticeable effects of **drastic weight loss** is the alterations in facial aesthetics. As the body sheds fat stores, it often leads to a **reduction in facial volume**, resulting in a **sunken appearance.**

What is expected for someone to notice from the face is, the cheekbones may become more pronounced, jawlines sharper and the overall facial contour may look more sunken. Another impact is you may realize the excess skin appears sagging, particularly around the cheeks, chin and neck, giving you a prematurely aged appearance.

## The Impact on Body Aesthetics

Beyond the face, significant weight loss can also impact the aesthetics of the body. **Loose skin, stretch marks and stubborn pockets of fats** are common concerns among individuals that have undergone weight loss.

Areas around your abdomen, thighs, arms and buttocks may show **laxity** and **lack of firmness**, making you look different and unattractive than you should from the weight loss journey.

## Treatment Approaches for Aesthetic Concerns Associated with Drastic Weight Loss

**Facial Volume Restoration**

Dermal fillers can help restore lost volume in the cheeks, temples and under eye areas, creating a youthful and rejuvenated appearance.

**Skin tightening**

Various non-invasive and minimally invasive procedures, such as HIFU, Fotona 4D and radiofrequency treatments, can stimulate collagen production and tighten loose skin. These treatments effectively improve skin elasticity and firmness reducing the appearance of sagging and wrinkles.

**Body Contouring**

Fotona TightSculpting has a unique laser system for sculpting and skin tightening on all body areas. It can be used to target stubborn fat deposits and excess skin. Minimally invasive procedures, cryolipolysis (CoolSculpting) are also one of the alternatives that can be used for body contouring means.

**Skin Rejuvenation**

Drastic weight loss has caused serious skin sagginess due to collagen loss. Some of the premium treatments to stimulate collagen productions are injectables like Plinest and Radiesse that stimulate the body to produce collagen from within, giving your skin a young, smoother and firm texture.

Drastic weight loss can bring about significant changes in facial and body aesthetics, often necessitating interventions to restore balance and harmony to one’s appearance.
