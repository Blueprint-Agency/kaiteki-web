## Introduction

The purpose of comparing **[Skinvive](/blog/choosing-your-skinboosters)** and **[Rejuran](/blog/rejuran-healer)** is to support better decision-making by helping patients and practitioners understand the key differences between these two skin treatments based on their goals, skin concerns, mechanism of action, and desired outcomes.

## Overview of Skinvive and Rejuran

### What is Skinvive?

Skinvive is an injectable skin booster made by Juvederm (Allergan Aesthetics) using **hyaluronic acid**. It is designed to improve **skin hydration**, smooth texture, and give a **subtle glow**. Suitable for dull, dehydrated, tired-looking skin, early signs of aging, and those wanting a natural glow.

### What is Rejuran?

Rejuran is a skin-rejuvenating injectable treatment using **polynucleotides (PN)** from **salmon DNA** to promote **cell regeneration**, healing, and **anti-aging effects**. Ideal for sensitive/inflamed skin needing repair, aging skin, and acne scars.

**Primary Benefits:**

-   Enhances skin elasticity and texture

-   Stimulates collagen production

-   Reduces fine lines, wrinkles, and acne scars

-   Strengthens skin barrier

-   Improves tone and radiance

-   Anti-inflammatory and healing properties

## Key Differences

| Feature | Skinvive (by Juvederm) | Rejuran (Rejuran Healer) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main Ingredient | Hyaluronic Acid (HA) | Polynucleotides (PN) from salmon DNA |
| Mechanism of Action | Provide moisture and improves skin smoothness from within | Stimulates skin regeneration, repair and healing, and collagen production |
| Primary Benefits | Skin hydration, glow | Skin repair, anti-aging, scar healing |
| Treatment Area | Cheeks (approved), off-label for other facial areas | Face, neck, under eyes, hands (versatile) |
| Longevity | ~6 months | 6–12 months (with maintenance) |
| Downtime | Minimal (redness/swelling may last 1–2 days) | Minimal (small bumps, swelling, or redness for 2–3 days) |

## Treatment Process and Experience

### Application Techniques

-   **Skinvive:** Injected as **microdroplets** more superficially for hydration

-   **Rejuran:** Injected with **microneedles** deeper for skin repair

Can be combined in one session.

### Downtime and Recovery

-   **Skinvive:** Mild redness/swelling, resolves in 24–48 hours

-   **Rejuran:** Redness, swelling, bumps up to 2–3 days
    Most patients resume activities same or next day.

### Patient Comfort

-   **Skinvive:** Contains **lidocaine**, minimal discomfort

-   **Rejuran:** Requires **topical numbing cream**; more stinging reported during injection

## Efficacy and Visible Results

### Expected Outcomes

-   **Skinvive:** Hydration, smoothness, glow

-   **Rejuran:** Collagen regeneration, healing, scar improvement

### Timeline for Improvements

-   **Skinvive:** Visible results **after 1 session**

-   **Rejuran:** Gradual improvement over **2–4 weeks**, usually needs **3–4 sessions**

### Longevity of Results

-   **Skinvive:** Lasts while hydration is maintained

-   **Rejuran:** Lasts **6–12 months** with maintenance

## Ideal Candidates and Indications

### Suitability for Skin Types

-   **Skinvive:** All skin types (not for pregnant/breastfeeding)

-   **Rejuran:** Damaged/sensitive skin, acne scars, aging skin

### Targeted Skin Concerns

-   **Skinvive:** Dehydration, dull skin, texture

-   **Rejuran:** Repair, aging, scars

### Age Considerations

-   **Skinvive:** Adults 18+ seeking hydration/glow

-   **Rejuran:** Adults in late 20s+ seeking repair/anti-aging

## Safety, Side Effects, and Risks

### Common Side Effects

-   **Skinvive:** Mild swelling, redness

-   **Rejuran:** Redness, swelling, bumps, bruising, itching

### Rare Complications

-   **Infection:** if hygiene is poor

-   **Lumps:** from poor injection technique

-   **Allergic reaction:** rare; **lower risk in Rejuran** due to DNA compatibility

### Precautions and Aftercare

-   Disclose allergies, conditions, medications before injection

-   Avoid **exercise, alcohol, sauna, sun** for 24–48 hours

-   Avoid **makeup, harsh products** for 24 hours post-treatment

-   Use **gentle skincare**, stay hydrated
