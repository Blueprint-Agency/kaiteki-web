## What is Fat Freezing Therapy?

Fat freezing, also known as cryolipolysis, is a non-invasive procedure that uses controlled cooling to target and eliminate fat cells. It’s FDA-approved and has become a popular alternative to liposuction for those looking to contour their bodies without surgery or downtime.

## How Does Fat Freezing Therapy Work?

Fat freezing works on the principle that fat cells are more sensitive to cold than surrounding tissues. Here’s how it works:

1.  **Targeted Cooling**: A specialized device is applied to the treatment area, cooling the fat cells to a temperature that causes them to crystallize and die.
2.  **Natural Elimination**: Over the next few weeks, your body naturally processes and eliminates the dead fat cells through the lymphatic system.
3.  **Visible Results**: The treated area becomes slimmer and more contoured, with results appearing gradually over 2-3 months.
