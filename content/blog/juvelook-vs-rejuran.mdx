## What Are Skin Boosters?

Skin boosters are rapidly gaining popularity in the aesthetics world due to their ability to hydrate, improve skin texture, repair the skin barrier, and boost collagen stimulation. Skin boosters work by delivering nourishing ingredients directly into the skin.

This minimally invasive treatment is especially favoured for its natural-looking results and effectiveness in rejuvenating dull, tired skin—making it a go-to option for those seeking a subtle yet noticeable anti-aging solution.

In this blog post, we are going to discuss two popular skin boosters originating from South Korea: Juvelook and Rejuran.

## What is Juvelook?

**[Juvelook](/blog/juvelook-skin-booster)** is an advanced skin booster designed to rejuvenate the skin by combining immediate hydration with long-term collagen stimulation. It consists of a dual-action formula of Poly-D,L-lactic acid (PDLLA) and Hyaluronic Acid (HA), providing gradual improvement in skin elasticity and texture.

This treatment is particularly effective for addressing signs of aging such as fine lines and wrinkles, as well as improving skin tone and reducing the appearance of acne scars. Unlike traditional fillers that primarily add volume, Juvelook works by stimulating the body’s natural collagen production (thanks to PDLLA!), leading to more natural and longer-lasting results.

Juvelook is suitable for individuals seeking a non-invasive solution to enhance their skin’s appearance. It is a particularly ideal option for those looking to achieve a youthful, radiant complexion without the need for surgical procedures.

The typical treatment regime is 2–3 sessions, spaced a month apart, with minimal to no downtime. Results last up to 6 months.

## What is Rejuran?

**[Rejuran](/blog/rejuran-healer)** is a polynucleotide-based skin booster derived from salmon DNA. These components are biocompatible with human skin and have regenerative properties that promote the repair of the skin at a cellular level, overall improving the skin barrier.

Its key benefits are to repair damaged skin (such as acne scars and sun damage), improve skin elasticity, and reduce inflammation and redness of the skin.

The number of sessions required for Rejuran is typically 3–4 sessions, spaced a month apart. The exact number of sessions depends largely on skin type and skin concerns. Downtime is minimal, and results last about 4–6 months.

## Which One Should You Choose?

**Choose Juvelook if:**

■ You want to improve rough, dry, dull skin
■ You are concerned about sagging skin
■ You wish for a natural firming, skin-tightening effect
■ You are concerned about fine lines, pores, and acne scars

**Choose Rejuran if:**

■ You need skin repair
■ You want to reduce the appearance of atrophic acne scarring
■ You want to reduce redness and inflammation
■ You want to improve skin elasticity

## Conclusion

Juvelook and Rejuran help with overall skin rejuvenation; however, they each provide benefits that are dependent on your skin type as well as specific individual concerns.

Juvelook combines an advanced dual-action of collagen stimulation from PDLLA and hydration from hyaluronic acid. This results in improved skin texture, hydration, reduction in the appearance of pores, acne scars, and also a reduction in fine lines and wrinkles.

Rejuran, on the other hand, focuses more on the repair of the skin at a cellular level, promoting skin regeneration. This provides improvement in acne scars, repairs damaged skin, and reduces inflammation and redness.
