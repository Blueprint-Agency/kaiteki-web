## What Is Belotero Revive?

Belotero Revive is a type of dermal filler used in aesthetic treatments, specifically designed to restore skin hydration, elasticity, and volume. It is part of the Belotero family of fillers, which are made from hyaluronic acid, a naturally occurring substance in the skin that helps to retain moisture and provide structure.

### Key Ingredients

-   Belotero Revive contains a cross-linked form of hyaluronic acid, which helps to attract and retain moisture in the skin, giving it a more hydrated, plump appearance.
-   Unlike traditional dermal fillers that are designed to fill wrinkles or volume loss, Belotero Revive is specifically formulated to improve skin quality. It can help smooth and hydrate the skin from within, resulting in a more radiant and youthful appearance.
-   It is typically used on the face, particularly around the cheeks, under the eyes, and on the décolletage, where skin can lose volume and elasticity due to aging.
-   When injected into the skin, Belotero Revive helps to restore moisture and improve skin quality by increasing hydration at a deeper level.
-   The results from Belotero Revive can last several months, typically between 6-12 months, depending on individual factors such as skin type, lifestyle, and the area treated.

## Benefits of Belotero Revive

-   Improved skin texture and elasticity
-   Smoother, more hydrated skin
-   Natural, refreshed appearance
-   Minimal downtime and quick recovery

## What Happens During Treatment?

### What to Expect During The Procedure

Before receiving any dermal filler, including Belotero Revive, you’ll have a consultation with a trained aesthetic doctor. The provider will discuss your goals for the treatment, such as improving hydration, smoothing fine lines, and restoring a more youthful appearance followed by assessing your skin’s condition and determine if Belotero Revive is appropriate for your needs.

The provider will check for any contraindications (e.g., allergies, certain medications, or conditions) that could affect the treatment.

### Prior to the treatment

While Belotero Revive contains lidocaine (a local anesthetic) to help minimize discomfort during the injection, your provider may also apply a topical numbing cream to the treatment area. This helps make the procedure more comfortable, especially if you’re sensitive to injections.

Your provider may mark specific areas of your face or body where the injections will be made, ensuring a targeted and precise treatment.

### How long Does the Treatment Take?

The actual injection of Belotero Revive usually takes about 20 to 30 minutes, depending on the areas being treated.

Belotero Revive is injected with a very fine needle or microcannula. You may experience a slight pinching or stinging sensation during the injection. However, the discomfort is generally mild and brief. The numbing agents help minimize pain.

## Aftercare tips

Belotero Revive is a minimally invasive treatment with little to no downtime. Most people can return to their regular activities right away. However, it’s helpful to follow these aftercare tips to ensure the best results:

-   -   **Avoid touching or massaging the treated area** for a few hours after treatment to prevent displacement of the filler.
    -   **Avoid strenuous exercise** and excessive heat (such as saunas or hot showers) for the first 24-48 hours after treatment.
    -   **Avoid alcohol and anti-inflammatory medications** (like aspirin or ibuprofen) for a few days, as these can increase the risk of bruising.
    -   **Apply makeup**: You can typically resume wearing makeup after 24 hours, though it’s a good idea to let any swelling or redness settle before doing so.
