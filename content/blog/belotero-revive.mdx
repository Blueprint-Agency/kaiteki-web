## What Is Belotero Revive?

[Belotero Revive](/technology/belotero-revive) is an injectable hyaluronic acid gel from Merz Aesthetics, used as a skin booster for dry facial skin and superficial fine lines. It carries the Belotero name, a range otherwise made up of dermal fillers, but it is not designed to add volume or contour. Hyaluronic acid is a naturally occurring substance in the skin that helps to retain moisture.

### Key Ingredients

-   Each millilitre contains 20 mg of cross-linked hyaluronic acid and 17.5 mg of glycerol, a water-binding humectant familiar from moisturisers. The two bind water in different ways, and the hyaluronic acid is of non-animal origin.
-   Unlike traditional dermal fillers that are designed to fill wrinkles or volume loss, Belotero Revive is formulated for skin quality. The manufacturer indicates it for dehydration and superficial fine lines on facial skin with early sun damage.
-   It is a facial product, most often used on the cheeks, the area most published studies treated. Its instructions exclude the area between the brows and the nose.
-   It is placed in the mid to deep dermis as many small deposits spread across the area, rather than shaped into a feature.
-   Any change is temporary, because hyaluronic acid is gradually broken down by the body, and how long it lasts varies between individuals.

## What Is Belotero Revive Used For?

-   Dry or very dry facial skin that feels tight or looks dull
-   Superficial fine lines linked to dryness rather than expression
-   Early loss of skin elasticity
-   Rough surface texture from accumulated sun exposure

It is not suitable for everyone, results vary between individuals, and a consultation determines whether it is appropriate for you.

## What Happens During Treatment?

### What to Expect During The Procedure

Before receiving Belotero Revive, you’ll have a consultation with a trained aesthetic doctor. The provider will discuss your goals for the treatment, such as improving hydration, smoothing fine lines, and restoring a more youthful appearance followed by assessing your skin’s condition and determine if Belotero Revive is appropriate for your needs.

The provider will check for any contraindications (e.g., allergies, certain medications, or conditions) that could affect the treatment.

### Prior to the treatment

Belotero Revive’s listed composition contains no lidocaine (a local anaesthetic), unlike some gels in the Belotero filler range, so your provider will commonly apply a topical numbing cream to the treatment area first and leave it to work. This helps make the procedure more comfortable, especially if you’re sensitive to injections.

Your provider may mark specific areas of your face where the injections will be made, ensuring a targeted and precise treatment.

### How long Does the Treatment Take?

The actual injection of Belotero Revive usually takes about 20 to 30 minutes, depending on the areas being treated.

Belotero Revive is injected with a very fine needle or microcannula. You may experience a slight pinching or stinging sensation during the injection. However, the discomfort is generally mild and brief. The numbing cream helps minimise discomfort.

## Aftercare tips

Belotero Revive is a minimally invasive treatment, and most people return to their regular activities soon afterwards. Small bumps, redness, swelling, tenderness and pinpoint bruising at the injection points are common and, according to the manufacturer, usually settle within a few days. The manufacturer’s aftercare is specific, and your doctor may adjust it for you:

-   **Avoid pressure, rubbing or strenuous exercise** straight after treatment.
-   **No make-up or skincare on the area** for at least 12 hours.
-   **Avoid saunas, steam rooms, peels, prolonged sun or UV exposure, and extreme heat or cold** for two weeks.
-   **Avoid alcohol** for 24 hours before and after treatment, and tell your doctor about blood-thinning medication, anti-inflammatory painkillers or supplements such as vitamin E or ginkgo, which can increase bruising.

As with any injectable, Belotero Revive carries risks, which are explained during consultation. Less common effects include inflammatory nodules, infection, pigment change and allergic reactions. Vascular occlusion, gel blocking a blood vessel, is rare but recognised with any facial injection: sudden or unusual pain, skin turning pale or any change in vision needs urgent attention.

## How Many Sessions Are Usual?

Changes, where they occur, develop gradually and vary between individuals, so no particular result or timeline can be promised.

**The manufacturer’s course**: one to three sessions at least four weeks apart, with the course repeated no sooner than six months later if it is continued. Your doctor sets the actual number and spacing for your skin and response rather than fixing them in advance.

**Combination approach**: If your concern is lifting, volume or contour, Belotero Revive is the wrong tool on its own. The manufacturer allows Revive and other Belotero gels in the same session in different areas of the face, so a doctor may combine it with a **filler** for volume, a decision made at consultation.

## Conclusion: Is Belotero Revive the Right Treatment for You?

Belotero Revive could be the right treatment for you if your main goals are to improve your skin’s hydration, texture, and radiance while addressing early signs of aging or skin dullness.

It is directed at mild concerns such as superficial fine lines and dryness, and it is not designed to add volume or lift. It is not used during pregnancy or breastfeeding, under the age of 18, or where there is infection or inflammation at the intended site. The manufacturer’s instructions also state there is no clinical data in the deepest skin tones (Fitzpatrick V and VI), and ask that patients of Asian background be told of a higher likelihood of reactions such as itching, swelling and redness.

In conclusion, if your focus is on skin texture, hydration and overall quality, Belotero Revive may be worth discussing with a doctor, who will tell you whether it suits your skin.
