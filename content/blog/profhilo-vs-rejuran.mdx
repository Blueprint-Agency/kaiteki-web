## Introduction

When it comes to achieving glowing, youthful skin, the world of aesthetic treatments offers a variety of options.

Two popular choices that have been making waves are **[Profhilo](/blog/profhilo)** and **[Rejuran](/blog/rejuran-healer)**. Both are injectable skin boosters designed to rejuvenate your skin, but they work in different ways and cater to different needs.

So, how do you choose between them? Let’s break it down and help you decide which one might be your skin’s new best friend.

## What Are Skin Boosters?

Skin boosters are injectable treatments that hydrate, plump, and rejuvenate the skin from within. Unlike traditional fillers that add volume to specific areas, skin boosters work by improving overall skin quality, texture, and elasticity.

They’re perfect for anyone looking to combat dryness, fine lines, or dullness and achieve a radiant, healthy glow.

## What is Profhilo?

Profhilo is a game-changer in the world of skin hydration. It’s made of hyaluronic acid (HA), a naturally occurring substance in the skin that retains moisture and keeps it plump.

What sets Profhilo apart is its unique formulation—it’s 100% pure HA with no additives, making it one of the most biocompatible treatments available.

### How Does Profhilo Work?

Profhilo is injected into the skin, where it spreads like a “bioremodeling” agent.

It stimulates collagen and elastin production, tightens the skin, and improves hydration levels.

The result? Firmer, smoother, and more radiant skin with a natural glow.

### Best For:

-   Deep hydration
-   Improving skin laxity (sagging)
-   Enhancing overall skin texture and radiance
-   Those with dry or aging skin

### Treatment Process:

Profhilo typically requires 2 sessions, spaced 4 weeks apart. Each session takes about 30 minutes, and there’s little to no downtime. Results can last up to 6 months.

## What is Rejuran?

Rejuran, on the other hand, is a skin booster that focuses on skin repair and regeneration. It’s made from polynucleotides (PN), which are derived from salmon DNA.

These PN molecules are highly compatible with human skin and work to stimulate healing and cell regeneration.

### How Does Rejuran Work?

Rejuran promotes collagen production, reduces inflammation, and accelerates skin repair. It’s particularly effective for improving skin elasticity, reducing fine lines, and healing damaged skin. Think of it as a reset button for your skin!

### Best For:

-   Repairing damaged or acne-scarred skin
-   Reducing fine lines and wrinkles
-   Improving skin elasticity and firmness
-   Those with sensitive or irritated skin

### Treatment Process:

Rejuran usually involves 3-4 sessions, spaced 2-4 weeks apart. Each session takes about 30 minutes, and downtime is minimal. Results can last up to 6-12 months, depending on your skin condition.

## Profhilo vs Rejuran: Key Differences

|   | **Profhilo** | **Rejuran** |

| **Main Ingredient** | Hyaluronic Acid (HA) | Polynucleotides (PN) |

| **Best For** | Dry, sagging, or dull skin | Damaged, acne-scarred, or aging skin |
| **Sessions Needed** | 2 sessions | 3-4 sessions |
| **Downtime** | Minimal | Minimal |
| **Results Duration** | Up to 6 months | 6-12 months |

## Which One Should You Choose?

The choice between Profhilo and Rejuran depends on your skin concerns and goals:

**Choose Profhilo if:**
You’re looking for deep hydration, improved skin texture, and a radiant glow. It’s ideal for those with dry or sagging skin who want a quick refresh without much downtime.

**Choose Rejuran if:**
Your skin needs repair, whether from acne scars, fine lines, or damage. It’s perfect for those who want to improve elasticity and heal their skin from within.
