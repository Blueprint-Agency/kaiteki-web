## How Does Alma Hair Removal Work?

Alma’s **SOPRANO ICE™** uses **diode laser technology** that targets the hair follicles beneath the skin without damaging surrounding tissues.

Key Features:

-   **Gradual heating** for pain-free sessions
-   **In-motion technique** ensures even coverage
-   **Advance cooling systems that** keeps the skin protected throughout

Unlike IPL, which uses scattered light and can be less effective over time, Alma laser delivers focused energy directly into the follicle—making it more reliable and efficient.
