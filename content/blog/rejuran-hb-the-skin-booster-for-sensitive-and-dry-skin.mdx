### The functions of Polynucleotides are as such:

### 1\. Tissue Repair & Cell Regeneration

PN are DNA fragments (commonly derived from salmon) that are highly biocompatible with human skin. Once injected, they act as biological signals that:

-   Stimulate fibroblasts (cells responsible for collagen production)
-   Promote cell turnover and repair
-   Help restore damaged skin structure

### 2\. Collagen Stimulation

PN activates fibroblasts to produce:

-   Collagen
-   Elastin

This gradually improves:

-   Skin firmness
-   Elasticity
-   Fine lines and wrinkles

### 3\. Anti-Inflammatory Effect

PN has been shown to:

-   Reduce inflammation
-   Support wound healing

### 4\. Improved Skin Environment

PN helps improve the extracellular matrix (ECM)—the “scaffolding” of the skin:

-   Enhances skin strength and resilience
-   Improves overall skin quality and texture

**Hyaluronic acid** is no stranger in the aesthetic and skincare world. It has been well known to be a humectant and is able to draw in water into the skin. It also improves the skin barrier by reducing TEWL ( transepidermal water loss) and makes the skin feel smoother.

## How Is Rejuran HB Different from Other Rejuran Types?

The Rejuran range consists of different formulations designed to target specific skin concerns, although all of them work using polynucleotides (PN) to repair and rejuvenate the skin at a cellular level.

**Rejuran Healer** is the original and most well-known version. It focuses on overall skin regeneration, improving skin texture, elasticity, fine lines, and acne scars. It is typically used across the whole face for patients who want general anti-aging and skin healing benefits.

**Rejuran I (Eye)** is specially formulated for the delicate under-eye area. It has a lower viscosity, making it suitable for thin skin, and helps improve fine lines, dark circles, and skin laxity around the eyes without causing excessive swelling.

**Rejuran S (Scar)** is a more concentrated and thicker formulation designed specifically for acne scars and deeper skin damage. It is injected more focally into scarred areas to promote stronger tissue repair and collagen remodeling.

**Rejuran HB (Hydration + Brightening)** combines polynucleotides with hyaluronic acid, making it ideal for patients seeking improved skin hydration, radiance, and a healthy glow. Compared to Rejuran Healer, it is lighter and more focused on enhancing skin moisture and brightness rather than intensive repair.

In summary, while all Rejuran types work on skin healing, **Healer** is for overall rejuvenation, **I** targets the eye area, **S** is for acne scars, and **HB** is best for hydration and glow.
