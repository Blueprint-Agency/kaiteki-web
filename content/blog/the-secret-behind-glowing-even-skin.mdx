## What Does “Uneven Skin Tone” Actually Mean?

“Uneven skin tone” is actually a blanket term, it doesn’t refer to just one specific issue.

It can show up as:

-   Dark spots or sun spots
-   Melasma (patchy brown areas, usually on cheeks or forehead)
-   Post-acne marks (those stubborn dark spots after pimples)
-   Redness
-   Overall dull or tired-looking skin

These variations are often due to **melanin**, the pigment that determines your skin color. Sometimes, your skin produces too much melanin in certain areas, leading to those pesky dark patches.

## The Main Causes of Uneven Skin Tone

### 1.Sun & UV Damage

This is the biggest culprit. When your skin is exposed to UV rays, it produces more melanin to protect itself. Over time, this leads to: **Sunspots/ Dark patches/ Uneven pigmentation**

And here’s the tricky part: Damage can start in your 20s but only become visible years later.

### 2\. Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH)

Ever noticed a dark mark after a pimple heals? That’s PIH.

It happens when your skin produces excess pigment in response to inflammation from: Acne/ Rashes/ Bug bites

The darker your natural skin tone, the more noticeable these marks tend to be and they can linger for months.

### 3\. Hormonal Changes

Melasma is a common but stubborn form of pigmentation.

It’s often **triggered by: Pregnancy/ Birth control pills/ Hormonal fluctuations**

It usually appears as **symmetrical patches** on: Cheeks/ Forehead/ Upper lip

Pollution particles can penetrate your skin and create oxidative stress, which:

And unfortunately sun exposure makes it worse very quickly.

### 4\. Pollution & Environmental Stress

If you live or commute in busy cities, this one matters.

-   Speeds up pigmentation
-   Makes skin look dull
-   Weakens overall skin health

## Simple Daily Skincare Tips

Now that we know the culprits, let’s talk about solutions. The good news is, with some easy tweaks to your daily skincare routine, you can keep your skin looking its best!

-   **Sunscreen is your BFF**: Apply SPF 30 or higher every day, rain or shine.
-   **Gentle Exfoliation**: 2-3 times a week helps shed those dulling dead skin cells.
-   **Key Ingredients**:
    – Niacinamide → reduces pigmentation
     – Vitamin C → brightens
     – AHAs (like glycolic acid) → improve skin turnover
     – Tranexamic acid → especially helpful for melasma
-   **Stay Hydrated**: Moisturise to keep your skin plump and even.

##### What To Avoid

-   **Picking at Pimples**: It’s tempting, but it guarantees dark marks.
-   **Skipping Sunscreen**: This is a big no-no.
-   **Harsh Whitening Products/ Over exfoliating**: They can do more harm than good.
