## What are the side effects?

It is generally considered to be a safe procedure with minimal risk of side effects. However, as with any medical procedure, there is always a chance of experiencing some side effects. The possible side effects of the treatment include:

1.  Bruising: Some patients may experience mild bruising at the injection site, which should resolve within a few days.
2.  Swelling: Some patients may experience mild swelling at the injection site, which should resolve within a few days.
3.  Redness: Some patients may experience mild redness at the injection site, which should resolve within a few hours.
4.  Pain or discomfort: Some patients may experience mild pain or discomfort during or after the procedure.
5.  Lumps or nodules: In rare cases, patients may develop lumps or nodules at the injection site. These are usually harmless and will resolve on their own within a few weeks.
6.  Allergic reactions : In rare cases, patients may experience an allergic reaction to the hyaluronic acid in the Profhilo, which can cause itching, redness, and swelling.

It’s important to note that these side effects are usually mild and temporary. They should resolve on their own within a few days. If you experience any side effects that are severe or persistent, you should contact your treatment provider immediately.

It’s also important to note that you should inform your practitioner of any medical conditions you have, allergies, and any medications you are taking as they could be contraindicated with Profhilo.
