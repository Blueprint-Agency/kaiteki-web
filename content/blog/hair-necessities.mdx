## Hair PRP ( platelet rich plasma)

Platelet rich plasma have been a huge hit in the past decade. Our blood is made of two main components – red blood cells and plasma. The plasma contains white blood cells and platelets which are rich in growth factors. These growth factors play the role of messengers, signaling skin cells to function and have been used in medicine to treat a range of health issues like arthritis, aging skin etc.

Hence, it has also been proven that PRP helps in stimulative the activity of hair follicles and promoting new hair growth. Several studies have shown that PRP has been effective in increasing thickness of the hair shaft and stimulate hair growth.

Treatment can be done every 2 to 4 weeks depending on severity of hair loss.

## Mesotherapy

Mesotherapy consists of microinjections with doctor’s concoction of multivitamins and growth factors on the scalp to reach the hair root directly. Mesotherapy complements other hair treatments.

## Regenera Activa

This is a new stem cell hair growth treatment that is quite promising. It is also known as autologous- micro graft treatment. Sounds puzzling? Well, let me elucidate a little.

Regenera Activa is a well-established hair loss treatment that is approved by major health regulatory authorities internationally – FDA (US), CE (Europe).

The treatment uses “micro-grafts” harvested from your own healthy scalo and has minimal side-effects and no social downtime.

Yes, you may feel some minimal pain at the scalp area after it has been injected but anaesthetic cream can be applied to make the entire process comfortable. On top of that, there might be some dull aching pain and minimal bleeding after the injections which may last for 2-3 days. Fear not, so far, nobody jumped off from the treatment bed!

This treatment can be done once or twice in a year. Unlike PRP which may require many sessions, this is a great treatment for those who prefer a one off treatment!

## Laser therapy

Low-level laser therapy appeared to be safe and effective for hair growth in both men and women.

In clinical studies, the Theradome® helmet has achieved outstanding results and some of the beneficial effects which are expected are:

-   Slowing down of hair loss,
-   A thickening of hair follicle size for existing hair;
-   New growth of hair.
