## How Does the Ellanse Filler Work?

Ellanse’s mechanism of action is two-fold. Firstly, it instantly provides volume, smoothing out wrinkles and restoring facial contours. Secondly, and more importantly, it triggers the body’s natural collagen synthesis. Over time, this increased collagen production results in firmer, more youthful skin.

## The Science Behind Ellanse Filler

Ellanse is a biodegradable, synthetic filler primarily composed of Polycaprolactone (PCL) and Carboxymethylcellulose (CMC). PCL is a biocompatible material that provides structure and support to the skin, while CMC helps create a gel-like consistency. When injected into the skin, Ellanse acts as a volumizer, filling out wrinkles and creases while stimulating collagen production.

**Common Side Effects**

As with any medical procedure, Ellanse treatment may cause some temporary side effects, which typically subside within a few days to weeks. These may include:

-   Redness, swelling, and bruising at the injection site
-   Mild pain or discomfort
-   Itching or numbness
-   Lumpiness or firmness
