## What are Skin Boosters?

As the name suggested, skin boosters are treatments that help boost overall skin health by promoting hydration, collagen stimulation and skin rejuvenation.

These treatments are minimally invasive injectable procedures. Unlike fillers, skin boosters aim to improve skin quality without adding volume.

While there are many different skin boosters in the market, our topic today will focus on hyaluronic acid (HA) based skin boosters.

## What is Profhilo?

**[Profhilo](/blog/profhilo)** is a pure HA skin booster. It contains a total of 64mg HA in 2ml syringe, which is the highest concentration of HA skin booster in the market.

With special patented NAHYCO technology, it’s high molecular weight HA is excellent in holding large capacity of water, thus providing long-lasting hydration and restore volume, while its low molecular weight HA promotes collagen remodelling, stimulate collagen production to improve skin laxity and overall skin texture and radiance.

Profhilo injection uses 10 bio-aesthetic points (BAP) injection technique which is relatively comfortable with minimal downtime. Most clients require 2-3 sessions of treatment for the best result.

## What is Juvelook?

**[Juvelook](/blog/juvelook-skin-booster)** is a hybrid skin booster combining 42.5mg of Poly D,L-lactic Acid (PDLLA) and 7.5mg of HA.

While HA provides hydration, PDLLA stimulates collagen production for skin rejuvenation, reduces fine lines and improve overall skin texture. It is also excellent in pore reduction and scar improvement.

This skin booster injection has minimal downtime and provides long-lasting result up to 6 months. For optimal result, we recommend 2-3 treatment sessions.
