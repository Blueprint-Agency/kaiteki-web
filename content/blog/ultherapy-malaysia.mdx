## What is Ultherapy?

In recent years, Ultherapy in Malaysia have become increasingly popular among the non-invasive cosmetic procedures.

As we age, skin loses collagen and elastin, skin becomes less firm and excess skin appears around the jowl, smiles lines and underneath the chin.

Ultherapy is a non-invasive procedure that utilises micro-focused ultrasound (MFU) energy to stimulate collagen production and elastin in the deeper layers of the skin, with the purpose of lifting and tightening the skin of the face, neck and chest.

It is a non-surgical alternative to a surgical facelift which can be done as a “lunch-time” procedure with little to no downtime.

## what does ultherapy do for you?

Ultherapy targets deep layers of your skin and stimulates the production of collagen. Collagen is a protein that gives your skin structure and elasticity, so increased collagen production can help to:

-   Restore youthful appearance
-   Reduce saggy face and skin
-   Enhance facial features
-   Reduce appearance of fine lines
-   Tighten loose neck skin
-   Reduce double chin
-   Lift and tighten area around the eyes to maintain a younger look

## Ultherapy VS HIFU

HIFU and Ultherapy are like cousins in the world of non-surgical skin rejuvenation, both using ultrasound to target your skin’s depths. Here’s a breakdown of their key differences:

**Targeting:**

-   **Ultherapy:** Deeper reach, ideal for lifting and tightening, particularly on the face and neck. It even visualizes the treatment area for precise targeting.
-   **HIFU:** Can reach various depths, some reaching deeper than Ultherapy. It might also offer fat reduction for body contouring. However, it often lacks real-time imaging.

**Experience:**

-   **Ultherapy:** May cause some mild discomfort.
-   **HIFU:** Often described as virtually painless.

**Cost and Treatment:**

-   **Ultherapy:** Generally more expensive but typically requires just one session.
-   **HIFU:** Can be cheaper but might require multiple sessions.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Who is an ideal candidate?**

Ultherapy is best for patients who would like to correct mild laxity and mild sagging skin or areas of fine lines and wrinkles. This treatment is also an ideal way to prevent the aging process.

**Is Ultherapy painful?**

In general, Ultherapy does not cause pain. Due to the fact that ultrasound energy is used, there will be a heating sensation when the energy is delivered. While some areas (e.g. jaw area) may have more discomfort than others, it is generally well-tolerated by many. A numbing cream will also be applied to ease any discomfort felt. For those with a lower pain threshold, oral analgesia is offered.

**How long does an Ultherapy session take?**

Between 60-90 minutes, depending on which areas are targeted. For example, full face and neck might take 90 minutes excluding cleansing and numbing cream application.

**Are there any age restrictions for the treatment?**

Can you be too old for Ultherapy? The resounding answer is no. As long as you are in good health and your skin condition is good, there is really no age limit for Ultherapy.

**How soon can I see results after Ultherapy?**

Results can usually be seen within 2 weeks, with its full effect seen within 3-6 months.

**Are the results of Ultherapy permanent?**

Collagen in our body naturally decreases with time, so Ultherapy cannot be considered a permanent procedure. The effects of Ultherapy lasts 12 months, at which point a second treatment will be necessary to maintain the results.

**Can Ultherapy be combined with other treatments?**

Yes! Ultherapy can be combined with fillers, threads and lasers for optimal results. Consult with us to learn more about which procedures can be done concurrently in the same setting.

**What if I am not satisfied with the results?**

Generally, patients are satisfied with results after their first Ultherapy session. However, it must also be noted that results of Ultherapy is not instantaneous due to the fact that collagen stimulation takes at least 3 months to set in. After the full results start to show, a second treatment may be done.
