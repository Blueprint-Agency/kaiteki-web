## What is Ultherapy ?

-   Ultherapy is a **non‑surgical** cosmetic procedure using **focused ultrasound energy**, designed to lift, tighten, and firm loose or sagging skin.
-   It is FDA‑cleared and used in many countries. It’s often dubbed as the alternative to surgical facelifts for people who want less invasive options.
-   It delivers a whopping 95% satisfaction rate a year, and is scientifically backed by over 120 publications and 56 clinical studies.
