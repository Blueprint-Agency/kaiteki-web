## What Are Fotona Laser Treatments?

Fotona 4D or 6D treatment is a non-invasive laser facelift designed to provide comprehensive facial rejuvenation, tightening, collagen stimulation and lifting effects. It combines different laser modes and wavelengths to target various layers of the skin and address multiple concerns.

## What is Fotona 4D?

Fotona 4D is a groundbreaking non-surgical face lifting procedure to treat 4 distinct “dimensions” of the skin resulting in rejuvenated and natural-looking appearance.

It works by using different laser modalities to target various layers of skin from the inside out.

Fotona 4D is a 30-45 minute non-invasive laser procedure that causes almost no downtime or discomfort, allowing you to carry on with your daily activities as usual.

An immediate freshness and tightness of the face can be seen soon after the treatment, but the full effect unfolds gradually over a period of 2-3 months. One can expect continuing improvement for up to six months after treatment.

An average 4 to 6 sessions, once every 3 to 4 weeks, will be required for optimum results.

### Step 1:

Fotona 4D starts inside the mouth, where laser energy is used to immediately kick start the tightening process around the jawline and perioral region. This step will help to improve the fine lines like nasolabial folds and wrinkles, much like a filler, but naturally.

### Step 2:

Next, to complement the intraoral treatment, deeper imperfections and pigments are precisely targeted to achieve an overall uniform complexion.

### Step 3:

In step three, a brushing technique is used to safely and rapidly apply heating to create an overall tightening effect, resulting in more firm and lifted skin.

### Step 4:

Lastly, a light laser peel is done to improve the skin radiance and restore youthful texture.

## What is Fotona 6D?

Just as the ‘4’ in ‘4D’ stands for the number of steps required during treatment, the same goes for 6D.

Fotona 6D is an enhanced version of the 4D treatment, offering more targeted procedures and treatment options for advanced skin concerns.

It builds upon the principles and steps of Fotona 4D with two additional rejuvenation steps either the eye (Fotona Smooth Eye), neck or mouth area for a complete skin rejuvenation treatment based on your specific areas of concern.

With these additional steps, doctors now can target more layers of the skin for more extensive and longer lasting result.
