## Introduction

Just got your skin boosters done and already loving that glow? ✨ We get it your skin looks fresher, smoother, and more hydrated, and naturally… you want it to last as long as possible.

While skin boosters do a lot of the heavy lifting, what you do *after* your treatment plays a huge role in how long your results actually last. Think of it like this: the treatment sets the foundation, but your daily habits help maintain (or shorten) that glow.

In this guide, we’ll walk you through exactly how to prolong your results from the first 48-hour aftercare, to skincare habits, lifestyle tips, and when to plan your next session.

## How Long Do Skin Booster Results Actually Last?

Before we talk about how to make your results last longer, it’s important to know what to expect.

Right after your treatment, you’ll usually notice an immediate glow within 24–72 hours this comes from the hydration effect kicking in, giving your skin that subtle plump and dewy look.

The peak results typically appear around 3–4 weeks, when collagen production improves and the hyaluronic acid (HA) is fully integrated into your skin.

As for how long results last, it depends on the type of skin booster:

-   Profhilo: around 6–9 months
-   Rejuran: about 4–6 months
-   Juvelook: can last up to 18 months

That said, these are just general timelines. Your metabolism, lifestyle, and skincare routine can all affect how quickly your body breaks down the product.

## The First 48 Hours Matter Most What To Do Right After

The first two days after your treatment are crucial. This is when the product is still settling into your skin, so small mistakes can affect your results.

First, avoid touching or rubbing your face. It might feel tempting, especially if there’s slight swelling or bumps, but touching can irritate the skin or even shift the injected product.

You’ll also want to skip makeup for at least 24 hours. Your skin has tiny injection points that need time to heal, and applying makeup too soon can introduce bacteria.

Try to avoid heavy exercise as well. Sweating and increased heat can disrupt the treated areas and worsen swelling.

It’s also best to avoid alcohol and blood-thinning substances, as they can increase bruising and slow down healing.

Lastly, stay away from heat and direct sun exposure this includes saunas, steam rooms, and even long periods outdoors. Heat can cause inflammation and affect how well the product settles.

Don’t worry if you notice mild redness, swelling, or tiny bumps these are completely normal and usually settle within a day or two.

## Hydration Is Your Best Friend

If there’s one thing you remember from this article, let it be this: hydration is everything when it comes to skin boosters.

Most skin boosters contain hyaluronic acid (HA), which works like a sponge it attracts and holds water in your skin. But here’s the catch: it needs water to function properly.

That’s why drinking enough water daily is so important. If your body is dehydrated, your results may not last as long or look as plump.

On top of that, using a topical HA serum helps support hydration from the outside. Think of it as boosting your booster.

Just as important is what you *don’t* use. Avoid harsh skincare products that strip moisture, like strong exfoliants or alcohol-based toners, especially in the early days.

A simple tip: pair your HA serum with a good moisturiser to lock in hydration this helps keep your skin smooth and bouncy for longer.

## Sun Protection Is Non-Negotiable

If hydration is step one, then sun protection is step two no exceptions.

UV rays are one of the biggest factors that break down both collagen and hyaluronic acid in your skin. This means more sun exposure = faster fading results.

Using SPF 50 daily is a must even if it’s cloudy, even if you’re indoors near windows. UV rays can still penetrate and affect your skin.

This is especially important in Malaysia, where we’re close to the equator and exposed to strong UV levels all year round. It’s not a seasonal thing it’s daily protection.

If you’re outdoors, remember to reapply sunscreen every 2–3 hours. And don’t rely on sunscreen alone wear a hat, stay in the shade, or use UV-protective clothing when possible.

## Build a Skincare Routine That Supports Your Results

You don’t need a complicated routine just a smart and gentle one.

After your treatment, focus on products that support healing and hydration:

-   A gentle cleanser that doesn’t strip your skin
-   A hyaluronic acid serum
-   A fragrance-free moisturiser
-   SPF 50 every morning

At the same time, there are a few things you should avoid, especially in the first 1–2 weeks.

Strong actives like AHAs and BHAs (exfoliating acids) can irritate your skin while it’s still healing. Retinol can also increase sensitivity, which isn’t ideal right after injections.

Try to avoid abrasive scrubs or facial tools too your skin needs time to recover.

The good news? These ingredients aren’t off-limits forever. Once your skin has healed, you can slowly reintroduce them back into your routine.

## Lifestyle Habits That Quietly Sabotage Your Results

Sometimes, it’s not your skincare it’s your daily habits.

For example, poor sleep can affect your results more than you think. Your skin repairs itself at night, so consistently bad sleep can slow down collagen production.

Chronic stress is another hidden factor. When your body produces too much cortisol (the stress hormone), it can actually break down collagen.

Then there’s smoking, which reduces blood flow to the skin and speeds up aging this significantly shortens how long your results last.

And of course, alcohol. It’s dehydrating, can trigger inflammation, and disrupts your sleep quality all of which work against your skin.

We know these habits aren’t easy to change. But even small improvements  better sleep, more water, less alcohol can make a noticeable difference over time.

## Plan Your Next Session Before the Glow Fades

Here’s something many people don’t realise: skin boosters work best when you treat them as a long-term investment, not a one-time fix.

Instead of waiting until your results completely fade, it’s better to top up earlier. This helps maintain your results and even improve them over time.

Different boosters have different maintenance timelines:

-   Profhilo: every 6 months
-   Rejuran: every 4–6 months
-   Juvelook: up to 18 months

Some people also choose to combine treatments for better results for example, pairing Profhilo (for hydration) with Rejuran (for skin repair).

The best approach is to consult your doctor and create a plan tailored to your skin.
