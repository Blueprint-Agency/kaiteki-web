## What is CoolSculpting?

Coolsculpting is also known as fat freezing whereby it uses a patented technology to freeze the fat cells non-invasively and eliminate it naturally for a better , contoured body.

In this modern era, everybody is trying to get into a healthier lifestyle. We have pilates, yoga , dance classes etc as people are more conscious about health and their body shapes. Hence, there is an increasing demand for non-invasive body contouring treatments.

Coolsculpting has been invented since 2008 when scientists discovered that sucking on popsicles can cause dimples due to the process of cryolipolysis. Later that year, Zeltiq started their clinical trials for the technology and was awarded FDA clearance for the technology. It has then taken the world by storm and it is one of the most performed body treatments in the world.

## How CoolSculpting Works?

During Coolsculpting treatment, pockets of stubborn fat will go through apoptosis, a process of cell death when controlled cooling is applied on it. This is a process of cryolipolysis, cryo= cold, lipolysis = breakdown of fat cells. Over the course of a few months, the body will gradually eliminate the fats naturally.

## Preparing for your CoolSculpting Session

Since this is a simple procedure, you can just wear comfortable clothes to the clinic.

**Before Coolsculpting :**

1.  Avoid blood thinners and multivitamins as it may make you more susceptible to bruising
2.  Check your skin for any inflammation/ infections. Coolsculpting should not be done on any inflamed/ infected skin or open wounds.

## What Happens After CoolSculpting?

**What are Post-Treatment Effects?**

Right after the cooling cups and applicators are released, the treated area will be slightly reddish and hard. This is where experienced coolsculpting practitioners will massage the treated area. Studies show that massaging the treated area for two to five minutes after the procedure enhances the treatment’s efficacy and contributes to further fat reduction. The massage also rewarms that part of the body.

**What are the Downtime and Recovery Tips?**

There is not much downtime for coolsculpting as the procedure is completely non surgical. You can return to your normal activities after treatment. However, do expect a little tenderness over the treated area if you press on it.

Drink enough water to hydrate yourself during this whole time as elimination of these waste products will be faster.

## Risks & Side Effects

**You might experience some mild side effects.**

After the procedure, some might experience redness, tingling and tenderness over the treated area. You may also feel lumpiness, itching, aching or cramping. However, it should be noted that these potential side effects are mild and will resolve within days.

## How many treatments do I need?

Usually up to 20-25% of fat will be reduced in that treated area after a single treatment. Having said that, it also depends on the thickness of the fat and your expectations.

Your doctor will work with you to determine the expected outcome and the number of cycles you need.
