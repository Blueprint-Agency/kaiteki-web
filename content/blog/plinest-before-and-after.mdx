Plinest treatment is generally well-tolerated with minimal discomfort. A topical anesthetic can be applied to further reduce any sensation.

Results typically become visible within 2-4 weeks and continue to improve over the following months.

**How often should Plinest treatment be repeated?**

The frequency of treatment depends on individual needs and desired results. Typically, a series of 2-3 treatments spaced 4-6 weeks apart is recommended.

**Are there any side effects of Plinest treatment?**

Plinest treatment is generally safe with minimal side effects. Some patients may experience mild redness, swelling, or bruising at the injection sites, which typically subside within a few days.

Plinest treatment is suitable for individuals of all skin types and ages who are concerned about skin aging, fine lines, wrinkles, and loss of elasticity.
