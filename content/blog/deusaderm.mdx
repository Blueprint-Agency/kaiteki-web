## The Downside of Hyaluronic Acid Fillers

We are familiar with Hyaluronic Acid based fillers. While they give instant promising results, delayed complications such as swelling and water retention is common.

Hyaluronic acid has a strong affinity for water molecules, which makes it perfect for maintaining moisture and hydration.

However, when it is used in areas with poor lymphatic drainage, it can result in fluid retention and puffiness, leading to an overfilled appearance.

## What is Deusaderm?

Here’s why Deusaderm is gaining popularity as an ideal alternative approach to areas especially the undereye region and overfilled faces.

Deusaderm is an injectable purified collagen injection developed by Sunmax Biotechnology in Taiwan.  The purified collagen is very similar to human collagen which makes it very biocompatible and can help effectively restore the loss of collagen and improving skin quality naturally.

This product has been licenced to use for almost 20 years across Taiwan, China, Singapore, Philipines and millions of treatments have been completed.

## Key Advantages Over HA Fillers

Because it does not contain Hyaluronic Acid which is a water drawing hydrophilic compound, it reduces complications like swelling, overfilling and puffiness.

It is also milky white in colour, giving off an immediate concealer effect for dark eye circles and avoiding the potential Tyndall effect.

## How Does Deusaderm Differ from Traditional HA Fillers?

Traditional HA fillers like Juvederm, Restylane fillers are gel based injectables. They primarily work as a space filler, giving volume replacement with minimal stimulation of new collagen.

On the other hand, collagen based injectable like Deusaderm not only restores volume, but also regenerate new collagen resulting in improvement in skin quality
