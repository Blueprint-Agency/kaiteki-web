## Introduction

[**PicoSure**](/blog/picosure-laser) and **PicoWay** are both industry-leading picosecond lasers—the gold standard for treating pigmentation, acne scars, tattoo removal, and skin rejuvenation.

Picosecond lasers work by delivering ultra-fast bursts of energy in trillionths of a second. These short pulses shatter pigment particles into tiny fragments, allowing your body to eliminate them more effectively—with less heat, less tissue damage, and a faster recovery.

## How to Choose?

Your ideal laser treatment depends on:

-   **Skin concerns**

-   **Pigment depth**

-   **Treatment goals**

Let’s break down the differences:

## What is PicoSure?

PicoSure is the world’s first picosecond aesthetic laser. It uses the 755nm Alexandrite wavelength and delivers ultra-short pulses of energy in trillionths of a second – this shatters pigment without causing heat damage to surrounding tissue.

But what truly sets PicoSure apart is its Focus Lens Array, which has a unique energy distribution that creates microscopic areas of laser-induced optical breakdown (LIOB) in the [dermis](https://www.google.com/search?rlz=1C1VDKB_enMY1172MY1172&cs=0&sca_esv=618282510bff2174&q=dermis&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjh35b1m-aOAxUlUGcHHY_2DfwQxccNegQIIBAB), stimulating collagen and elastin production.

This makes it not just a pigmentation laser, but also a powerful tool for skin rejuvenation and anti-aging.

## What is PicoWay?

PicoWay is a multi-wavelength picosecond laser that operates at **532nm, 785nm and 1064nm**. These different wavelengths allow it to target pigment in various skin layers and also multi-coloured tattoos better.

## Compare the Differences

| Treatment Area | PicoSure | PicoWay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tattoo Removal | Best for **blue, green, and dark ink** | Best for **multi-colored tattoos** |
| Skin Rejuvenation | **Focus Lens** tech → strong collagen remodeling, **minimal downtime** | Less targeted collagen stimulation |
| Acne Scar Treatment | **Focus Lens Array** | **Resolve fractional handpiece** |
| Downtime | Minimal (mild redness lasting a few hours) | Slight redness/swelling, may last up to 24 hours |
| Pain Level | Comfortable, mild warmth | Slightly more intense, but still tolerable |

## Potential Risks

Both PicoSure and PicoWay have similar potential risks:

-   Some redness, swelling

-   Blistering or scabbing

-   Hyperpigmentation or hypopigmentation

-   Skin sensitivity or irritation

## Sessions and Frequency

The number of sessions depends on the indications and skin condition. Both picosecond lasers can be repeated once every month.
