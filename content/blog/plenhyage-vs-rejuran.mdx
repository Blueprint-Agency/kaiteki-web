## Introduction

Here is the honest short answer before we get into detail. Plenhyage and Rejuran are both polynucleotide skin boosters made from purified salmon DNA, and they do broadly the same job: calm the skin, boost hydration, and nudge your body to rebuild collagen.

Rejuran is the Korean original with the longest track record. Plenhyage is a newer Italian option that clinics often market as stronger or more concentrated. That marketing line is where most of the confusion starts, so this guide separates what is genuinely proven from what is sales talk, and helps you decide based on your skin and your budget rather than the hype.

## What Is Rejuran?

Rejuran is built around polynucleotides, which are healing fragments of DNA taken from salmon. Those fragments, roughly 50 to 300 base pairs long, act like a repair signal once they are injected into the upper layers of your skin. They calm inflammation, improve hydration, and gradually stimulate collagen, the protein that keeps your skin firm and bouncy.

Because it is gentle and soothing, Rejuran is a favourite for sensitive or reactive skin, dull texture, and tired looking under eyes, where a dedicated version is used for that thin, delicate area. It has been on the market the longest of the polynucleotide boosters, so providers have years of real world experience with it. If you want the bigger picture first, our overview of [skin booster treatments](/blog/skin-booster) sets the scene well.

## What Is Plenhyage?

Plenhyage is also a salmon derived polynucleotide booster, so it sits in exactly the same family as Rejuran. It comes from Italy and is usually offered in two strengths, a medium version and a stronger XL version. The stronger version is often positioned for more intensive concerns such as acne scarring, post laser recovery, and deeper lines.

You will frequently see clinics quote a high polynucleotide dose per session, sometimes around 50mg, and describe Plenhyage as more comfortable to inject. It is worth being clear eyed here: those figures and comfort claims come from suppliers and clinics rather than independent published trials, so treat them as marketing positioning, not settled fact. The underlying science is the same proven polynucleotide repair mechanism that Rejuran uses.

## Marketing vs What Is Actually Proven

This is the part most blogs skip. The strongest, best documented product of the two is Rejuran, simply because it has been studied and used for longer. However, there is no real best product in the market. Each product has its own merits.

A higher dose on paper does not automatically mean a better result on your face, and more product is not always better for delicate areas. Likewise, the claim that one stings less than the other is highly dependent on technique, numbing, and the injector’s skill.

None of this means Plenhyage is bad. It is a genuinely good booster. It just means you should weigh the actual evidence rather than the brochure, and trust an experienced injector over a marketing line

## Which One Should You Choose?

Match the booster to your main concern. If your skin is sensitive, reactive, or you mainly want to refresh dull texture and tired under eyes, Rejuran and its long track record are a safe, well understood choice. If your focus is acne scarring, recovery after laser, or deeper lines and you want a more intensive option, the stronger Plenhyage version is reasonable to consider.

Budget matters too, since a full course is several sessions. Be honest with yourself about what you are treating and how much you want to spend across the whole plan rather than per visit, because that is what really shapes value.

## What Results and Downtime Really Look Like

Set realistic expectations, because these are gradual treatments, not instant fixes. On the day, expect tiny bumps where the product was placed, plus some redness or mild swelling. For the first one to three days those bumps settle and any redness fades.

Around week four, after a session or two, most people start to notice skin that looks fresher and feels more hydrated and bouncy. The fullest effect tends to land around the three month mark once collagen has had time to rebuild. Results are not permanent.

Most people maintain them with a top up every six to twelve months. Keep the skin clean and moisturised afterwards, skip heavy makeup for a day, avoid saunas and intense workouts briefly, and wear sunscreen.
