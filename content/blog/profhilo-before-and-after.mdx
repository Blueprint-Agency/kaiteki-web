**Is Profhilo safe?**

Yes, Profhilo is FDA-approved and has undergone rigorous testing to ensure its safety and effectiveness.

**How long does a Profhilo treatment take?**

A Profhilo session typically takes around 15-30 minutes.

**How often should I get Profhilo treatments?**

For optimal results, it is recommended to undergo a course of 2-3 Profhilo treatments spaced 4-6 weeks apart. Maintenance treatments can be done every 6 to 9 months to sustain the rejuvenating effects.

**What are the side effects of Profhilo?**

Common side effects include mild redness, swelling, or bruising at the injection sites, which typically resolve within a few days.

**Who should not get Profhilo treatments?**

Profhilo is not recommended for individuals with active skin infections, allergies to hyaluronic acid, or unrealistic expectations.
