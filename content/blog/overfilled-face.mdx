## The Puffy Pillow Face

The puffy pillow face used to be called as the Hollywood syndrome. No doubt fuller cheeks make you look younger but overly puffed up cheeks give you an overstuffed pillow appearance and sunset eyes ( eyes squished and end up looking smaller). It’s not sexy

## The Duck Lips

You know this one. Lips are one of the most common areas where fillers tend to go overboard, leading to  over-plumped lips. This can also happen if you receive the wrong range of filler or if you fall into the wrong hands. You can avoid this by making sure that you only see a trained and experienced injector.
