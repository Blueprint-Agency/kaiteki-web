The science of the lift

It works where creams can't reach.

Most treatments only work on the top of the skin. Ultherapy sends focused ultrasound energy deep down to the SMAS layer, the same layer a surgeon lifts in a facelift. This makes your body build new collagen.

  1. Skin check & ultrasound imaging

    Your doctor scans your skin layers in real time to find the exact depth for the treatment.

  2. Energy delivered to the SMAS

    Focused ultrasound reaches the same deep tissue a facelift works on, with no cutting.

  3. New collagen builds up

    Your skin starts to rebuild from within. New collagen forms slowly over the next few weeks and months.

  4. Your natural lift shows

    A firmer jawline, lifted brows and tighter neck appear over time. You look fresher, not different.
